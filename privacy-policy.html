Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Short version: This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any data about you or your browsing. Everything stays on your device.

What data we collect

None. YT Dark Video does not collect any personal data, browsing history, or usage statistics.

What we store locally

The extension uses browser.storage.local (your device only) to remember two settings:

  • Whether dark mode is enabled (on/off)
  • Which filter preset you last used (Smart, Warm, Full, Matrix, Cool)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to anyone other than you.

Network requests

This extension makes zero network requests. It does not contact any external server, analytics service, or API. All processing happens locally in your browser using GPU-accelerated SVG filters.

Permissions explained

  • storage — to save your on/off and preset preferences locally
  • *://*.youtube.com/* — to inject the dark filter on YouTube pages only

No other permissions are requested or used.

Third-party services

None. The extension does not integrate with any third-party service, analytics platform, or advertising network.
